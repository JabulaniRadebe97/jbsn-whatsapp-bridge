Data Deletion Instructions

JBSN Creative · Last updated: 13 October 2026

You may request deletion of personal information that JBSN Creative holds about you.

How to submit a request

  1. Email radebeju97@gmail.com with the subject line Personal Data Deletion Request.
  2. Tell us which contact method or service you used (for example, WhatsApp) and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant records. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.
  3. We may contact you to verify your identity before processing the request.

What happens next

We will review the request and delete or de-identify personal information we can lawfully remove. Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, to resolve disputes, or to maintain required business records. If any information cannot be deleted, we will explain the reason where appropriate.

We aim to respond within a reasonable period and will contact you if we need additional information. This page explains how to request deletion of information held by JBSN Creative; it does not delete information held independently by third-party services such as Meta/WhatsApp. You may need to submit a separate request to those services.
